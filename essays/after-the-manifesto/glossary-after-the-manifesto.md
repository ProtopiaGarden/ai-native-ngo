# Glossary — "After the Manifesto" (RU → EN → UA)

Translation contract for the essay. Use these renderings consistently across EN and UA so the
text matches the terminology already published in the AI-native NGO white paper.

**Note on sources (2026-06-16):** the live EN `white-paper/README.md` in the repo is currently a
14-line stub, **not** the full English text, so EN terms could not be validated against a full
live EN canonical. EN terms here were set against the live **RU** (`CONCEPT-RU.md`) and **UA**
(`CONCEPT-UA.md`) source-of-truth files and the author's corrections — notably *объект
воздействия → object of intervention* (not "object of impact": "impact" is reserved for the
result/claim layer), and UA *машиночитний* (matching the live UA white paper, not "машиночитаний").
The EN canonical white paper should be restored before this glossary is treated as EN-validated.

**Register note:** plain, patient, explanatory prose for a wide non-expert reader; every term is
introduced in ordinary language. Not academic, not marketing-glossy.

**Claim hygiene:** no "first / nobody / unique / revolutionary / head and shoulders above."
Novelty = assembling known things into a different object. Name neighbours honestly.
